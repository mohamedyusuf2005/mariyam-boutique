The Mariyam Heritage

Where Indian Royalty Meets Contemporary Poise

Founded on a devotion to slow luxury, Mariyam Boutique revives centuries of forgotten karigari into modern heirloom silhouettes.

Each piece is born in our ateliers across Varanasi, Chanderi, and Jaipur, where master generational weavers spin pure silken filaments with genuine gold and silver zari. We believe that true luxury lies in patience, provenance, and effortless grace.

From regal bridal lehengas that carry the emotional weight of a lifetime to crisp silk kurtis tailored for contemporary soirées, Mariyam crafts memories to be cherished across generations.
